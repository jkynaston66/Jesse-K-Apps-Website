A modern CRM for real estate

Real estate CRM for Arizona—and beyond.

JK Connect is being designed to help real estate agents and teams organize contacts, understand every lead, remember the next follow-up, and work together from one focused relationship hub.

Built with Tucson and Arizona real estate workflows in mind, while designed to support professionals nationwide.

Honest product status

What exists today—and what is still ahead.

✓Working in the internal preview

  • Fictional contact, lead, note, and task records
  • Search, navigation, record editing, and validation
  • Dashboard summaries and adaptive phone / tablet layouts
  • Local preview security and session boundary testing

→Still under development

  • Approved production CRM data connection and persistent synchronization
  • Live reminders, shared activity history, and team collaboration
  • Property search and IDX integration
  • AI-assisted production workflows
  • Live messaging and connected communication channels

Passing internal preview tests does not mean the product is released, connected to production systems, or ready for customer data.
