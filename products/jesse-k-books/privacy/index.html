Privacy Policy

How Jesse K Books stores and handles local data, backups, accounts, and optional services.

About Jesse K Books

Jesse K Books is provided and operated by Jesse K Apps, LLC. It is accounting and business-management software designed for business users throughout the United States. The Software is intended only for people who are at least 18 years old and is not directed to children.

Required account and registration

Jesse K Books requires an account before the application can be used. Account and registration information—including first and last name, email address, phone number, profession, company, city and state, product and platform details, installation identifier, and Terms and Privacy acceptance records—may be transmitted to and stored by or for Jesse K Apps for account administration, support, product administration, and registration tracking.

Firebase Authentication is used for email-and-password sign-in and password recovery. Firebase handles authentication credentials under Google’s and Firebase’s services. Jesse K Books does not store passwords in its local database or registration spreadsheet.

Data stored on your device

Your accounting records, transactions, journal entries, accounts, categories, customers, invoices, receipts, documents, mileage, payroll information, contractor and W-9 status information, settings, preferences, and business profile data are primarily stored locally on your device. Registration identity information is separate from those records. Jesse K Books does not centrally receive or store accounting records, payroll, receipts, customers, invoices, mileage, contractor or W-9 information, or Google Drive backup contents unless you explicitly send information through a support or contact workflow.

Contractor lists display only a masked TIN ending. This release stores only the TIN type and last four digits, not the full TIN. User-created accountant exports may contain contractor contact information and payment summaries; you choose where those exports are saved and whether to share them.

Payroll privacy

Payroll calculations use information you enter or configure. Jesse K Books intentionally does not store full employee Social Security numbers, full contractor taxpayer identification numbers, W-4 scans, I-9 scans, W-9 scans, employee bank credentials, or government-site credentials. Enter required full identifiers directly into the applicable official filing system.

Generated paystubs, payroll worksheets, PDFs, and exports contain financial information. You choose where exports are saved and are responsible for protecting them. Google Drive Backup and Device Sync remain subject to the disclosures in this policy.

Local receipt and document text recognition

Jesse K Books may use optical character recognition (OCR) technology provided by your device’s operating system, such as Apple Vision on supported Apple devices or Windows Media OCR on supported Windows devices, to extract text from receipts or documents. In the current implementation, this OCR processing occurs locally on your device; receipt images are not uploaded to Jesse K Apps for artificial-intelligence processing through this feature. OCR output may be incomplete or inaccurate. You are responsible for reviewing and correcting extracted information before relying on it or recording it in the Software. References to operating-system technologies identify compatibility only and do not imply endorsement of Jesse K Books by Apple, Microsoft, or any other provider.

Portable backups

A manual backup is a portable version-2 ZIP file. You choose where it is saved. It can contain sensitive business, financial, contractor, W-9-status, and locally attached document information, so you are responsible for protecting the file and every location where you copy it.

Optional Google Drive backup and Device Sync

You must explicitly connect your own Google account. Jesse K Books requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission, which lets the app create and manage backup and sync files it creates. Backup ZIPs and Device Sync files are stored in your connected Google Drive. You can turn automatic backup on or off and disconnect Google Drive at any time.

Google Drive storage is governed by your Google account and Google’s policies. OAuth access and refresh credentials are stored using operating-system-protected credential storage. Jesse K Books does not send these files to Jesse K Apps, Jesse Kynaston, NEXA, OpenAI, or unrelated third parties. Account deletion does not automatically delete Jesse K Books Backups or Jesse K Books Sync files. Those files remain under your control in Google Drive and can be deleted manually in Google Drive if you choose.

Data minimization

Jesse K Apps seeks to collect only the personal information reasonably needed to provide accounts, administer the product, support users, maintain security, document legal acceptance, and operate features you choose. The Software is designed so that accounting, payroll, customer, invoice, receipt, mileage, contractor, and document data remains primarily on your device.

Profession-targeted promotions

Jesse K Books may use the profession selected in your account to choose promotional content shown inside the Software. This targeting occurs locally on your device. Viewing or dismissing a promotion does not send your accounting or financial records to Jesse K Apps or the mortgage advertiser. The Software stores limited impression and action metadata locally to manage rotation. If you select Call Jesse, Text Jesse, Email Jesse, or another external action, your device opens the applicable third-party service.

Mortgage calculator

Mortgage calculator results are informational estimates only. They are not a loan approval, commitment, disclosure, rate lock, guarantee, or promise of particular terms. Calculator scenarios are not sent to Jesse unless you explicitly choose a contact or application action.

Software and mortgage roles are separate

Jesse K Apps, LLC provides the Jesse K Books software. Mortgage advertisements and mortgage services shown in the Software are provided separately by the identified mortgage professional and licensed mortgage company. Jesse K Apps does not make mortgage credit decisions, receive mortgage applications through the Software, or use local accounting data to determine mortgage eligibility.

External services and processing locations

Optional or user-initiated services include Firebase Authentication, Google Drive, your device’s email, SMS, and phone handlers, and external mortgage destinations opened by a link or QR code. Those providers control their own privacy and security practices. Information handled by Firebase or Google may be processed or stored in the United States or other locations where those providers operate, subject to their terms and safeguards.

Analytics, telemetry, and artificial intelligence

Jesse K Books does not include undisclosed analytics or telemetry. Jesse K Apps does not use accounting records, payroll data, customer data, documents, backups, or other local business data to train artificial-intelligence models. The Software does not send that local data to an artificial-intelligence provider unless a future feature clearly explains the transmission and you affirmatively choose to use it.

Operational communications and marketing

Jesse K Apps may send communications reasonably necessary to administer your account, protect security, respond to support requests, provide legally required notices, or explain material service changes. Creating an account does not provide consent to unrelated marketing email, text messages, or calls. Any future marketing consent must be requested separately where required.

Disclosures for service, law, security, and business transfers

Jesse K Apps may disclose limited personal information to service providers that help operate authentication, account administration, support, security, or other features you choose; when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, a valid subpoena, court order, or other enforceable legal process; to investigate fraud, abuse, unlawful activity, or security threats; to protect users, the public, Jesse K Apps, or others; or in connection with a legitimate merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale or transfer of Jesse K Apps or Jesse K Books. Advance notice may not be provided when prohibited by law. A business transfer is not the sale of personal information as a standalone data product, and transferred information remains subject to this policy and applicable law.

Security incidents

Jesse K Apps may take reasonable protective actions after suspected compromise, including temporarily restricting an account, requiring a password reset, revoking sessions or tokens, disconnecting integrations, and notifying users. No system can guarantee detection of every incident. Jesse K Apps will notify affected users of qualifying security incidents when required by applicable law.

Support communications

If you contact support, Jesse K Apps receives the information you choose to include, such as your contact details, message, screenshots, diagnostics, or attached files. Do not send passwords, full Social Security numbers, full taxpayer identification numbers, government-site credentials, or unnecessary financial records. Support information may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain security, document the request, and meet legal obligations.

Your privacy choices and requests

Users throughout the United States may request access to, correction of, or deletion of centrally held account and registration information by contacting support@jessekapps.com from the registered email address. Jesse K Apps may take reasonable steps to verify identity and authority before acting. Verified deletion requests generally will be completed within 30 days, unless additional time or retention is reasonably necessary or permitted by law. Jesse K Apps may retain limited legal-acceptance, security, fraud-prevention, transaction, support, and compliance records where reasonably necessary.

Deleting centrally held account information does not delete accounting or business data stored on your device, Google Drive backup files, Device Sync data, user-created exports, or copies held by third parties. You must separately delete those items from each location you control. Residual copies may remain temporarily in service-provider backups until ordinary backup rotation completes.

Security and data sales

Jesse K Books uses reasonable local and operating-system protections, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device password, backups, Google account, and operating-system credentials. Jesse K Apps does not sell users’ personal or business data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the Software, law, services, and data practices change. The current revision date appears above. Minor clarifications and contact corrections may be posted without unnecessary re-acknowledgment. Material changes affecting how personal information is collected, used, disclosed, or shared will receive clear notice and fresh acknowledgment or consent where appropriate or legally required. Material revisions are versioned separately from the Terms of Use / User Agreement.

Privacy and software questions may be sent to support@jessekapps.com or mailed to Jesse K Apps, LLC, 7090 N Oracle Rd, Ste 178 PMB 1265, Tucson, AZ 85704.
