Jesse K Books Privacy Policy

How Jesse K Books stores and handles local data, backups, accounts, and optional services.

On this page Your books and other people’s records Sign-in and registration Backups and exports you choose Who may receive information Access and deletion requests Contact and related notices

Revision dated November 18, 2026

Who this policy covers

Jesse K Apps, LLC provides Jesse K Books for adults in the United States. This notice covers the information we handle for the product. The Android Privacy Policy gives additional details for Android and controls for Android-specific features. Our website has a separate notice. A feature available in a private test or another edition may be unavailable on your device.

Your books and other people’s records

Books keeps the ordinary working copy of your accounts, transactions, journal entries, invoices, customer details, receipts, mileage entries, business settings and supported payroll or contractor records on your device. These records can identify employees, customers and vendors. A business that enters someone else’s data controls what it records and exports. If your employer or another business keeps your information in its own books, contact that business to request changes to its copy. You may also contact us about information we hold.

Sign-in and registration

Where an account is required, Google Firebase Authentication handles email and password sign-in, account identifiers, recovery and technical information used to secure access, such as IP address and device or service identifiers. Jesse K Apps does not store your plaintext password in the accounting database or registration sheet.

On editions with registration, we may receive your name, email, phone, profession, company, city and state, app and platform information, installation ID, registration timestamps and separately recorded Terms and Privacy versions. We use these records to administer the account, provide support, prevent abuse and document acceptance. Google-hosted registration records are separate from your accounting database. Signing in to an existing account on Android is described in its supplement. Required account processing is separate from an optional backup connection.

Payroll and sensitive documents

Payroll data you enter can include wages, withholding, tax elections and pay history. A designated masked contractor field uses TIN type and the last four digits. Full identifiers, withholding and identity documents, and bank or government credentials do not belong in these fields. A photo, file or note that you add can still contain sensitive information. Review and redact it as appropriate before attaching, exporting or sending it.

Receipt capture and recognition

Receipt recognition reads an image to suggest entries such as the merchant, date, tax or total. Review the original before posting. Supported desktop readers use Apple Vision or Windows Media OCR locally on supported files. Android uses a bundled Google ML Kit reader. Receipt contents stay on the device for local recognition. The Android supplement describes separate SDK diagnostic traffic. Local recognition does not send business records to an external AI model. Jesse K Apps does not use your bookkeeping records, receipts, payroll, exports or backups to train AI models. If we introduce a service that sends receipt contents to a remote provider, we will first explain what is sent, to whom and why, and ask you to choose whether to use that feature.

Backups and exports you choose

A manual backup can contain the database and attachments. Reports, accountant exports, payroll documents and spreadsheets can expose names, amounts, addresses or identifiers in readable form. You choose the destination and who may open each copy. Desktop portable ZIP files and Android password-protected backups have different formats. Treat every export as sensitive. A file checksum detects some changes to a file and does not protect its contents from someone who can read it.

If your edition offers Google Drive backup and you connect it, the feature writes backup files to the Google account you select. The desktop integration uses per-file Drive access for files created or selected for the app. Disconnecting stops future access through the connection as provided by the service. It does not erase files you have saved. Remove files and manage access in the selected storage account. Where automatic Drive backup is available, you may turn it off separately. Choosing a storage destination through a system file picker is a separate action. Production Device Sync is disabled for the editions covered here. We do not maintain a central copy of your ledger through that feature. The desktop Drive integration uses the drive.file scope and operating-system credential storage for the connection. You can revoke access in your Google account. We use access to your Drive files to deliver the storage feature you choose. We do not use Google Drive data to select or serve mortgage advertisements.

Mortgage ads and contact choices

Where included, Books may use your selected profession on your device to choose an in-app promotion for Jesse Kynaston’s mortgage services through the identified licensed company. Limited display and action history is used locally to rotate promotions. Viewing or dismissing a pop-up does not send your books, receipt contents or calculator scenario to the advertiser. You can use Books without making a referral or contacting the advertiser.

If you deliberately call, text, email, share a worksheet, scan a code or apply, your chosen handler or destination may receive your contact details, what you send and ordinary connection information. Review any prefilled message or attachment before sending. Creating a Books account or viewing a promotion does not enroll you in mortgage marketing. Jesse K Apps does not make lending decisions from your books.

Support and service messages

We receive the message, contact information, screenshots, diagnostics and files you choose to send to support. That choice can give us a copy of financial information otherwise kept only by you. Redact unnecessary information. Do not email passwords, backup keys, full tax identifiers or unredacted payroll files. We use these materials to answer your request. We may send operational messages about recovery, security, support, legal notices or material product changes. We request separate marketing permission when required. Where a desktop edition checks for updates, the update host may receive ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and request time. App stores may also handle update and download activity under their own policies.

Who may receive information

Service providers may process limited data for sign-in, registration, hosting, security, diagnostics and support. A store, Google Drive or another destination you select may receive the information needed for your action. Providers may process information in the U.S. or other countries where they operate. We may disclose relevant information to comply with lawful process, investigate fraud, respond to security incidents, protect rights, or complete a business transfer subject to applicable law and existing privacy commitments. We do not sell personal or business data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention and security

Local records remain under your control and may also exist in device backups, exported files or storage accounts. Sign-in and registration information is used while needed to maintain the account. Support materials are retained as needed to resolve and document the request. Limited acceptance, security and deletion records may be kept as reasonably necessary for compliance, fraud prevention or disputes. Provider backup copies may expire through normal rotation. We will notify affected people of qualifying security incidents as applicable law requires. Protect your device, email, storage credentials and exports.

Access and deletion requests

U.S. users may ask us to access, correct or delete information we hold. Email support@jessekapps.com using the account address if possible. If you lost that mailbox, say so and we will offer reasonable alternative verification. An authorized representative may contact us with proof of authority. We will complete a verified deletion request within 30 calendar days, or sooner if required by law. If applicable law permits or requires more time or specific retention, we will explain the reason and timing when permitted. We will address any further rights, including an applicable appeal, under the law that covers your request.

Account deletion ends sign-in. Make and test any required backup or export first. We cannot remotely erase copies you keep on devices, in your Google Drive or another destination, or with an accountant. Remove those copies separately if appropriate, after meeting your recordkeeping obligations. The Android deletion page gives device-specific steps.

Children and policy changes

Books is for adults. We do not direct it to children under 13 or knowingly collect an account from a child under 13. Contact us if a child supplied account information. We will identify policy revisions, notify you of material changes and obtain new consent where required before materially different processing begins.
