FAQ

Jesse K Books questions.

Is Jesse K Books really free?

Yes. Jesse K Books is 100% free to everyone.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription is required.

Where is my accounting data stored?

Normal accounting records are primarily stored locally on your device.

Does Jesse K Apps store my books?

Jesse K Apps does not centrally store your normal accounting records or accounting database. Limited account, authentication, support, and service information may be handled separately.

How does Google Drive backup work?

If you enable backup or Device Sync, files are stored in the Google Drive account you connect. Review the Privacy Policy for complete details.

Does Jesse K Books work on Mac and Windows?

It is being prepared for both Mac and Windows. Public installers are not available yet.

Is there an iPhone or iPad version?

Jesse K Books for iPhone and iPad is coming soon.

Can I import transactions?

Jesse K Books supports importing supported records. Review imported information before relying on it.

Can I export my records?

Yes. You control exported files and where you choose to send or store them.

Is Jesse K Books tax or accounting advice?

No. It is recordkeeping software and does not provide tax, accounting, financial, or legal advice.
