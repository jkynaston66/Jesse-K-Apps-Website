01

Local business records

Transactions, accounts, receipts, reports, and other normal accounting records are primarily stored on your device.

02

Backups you choose

Save portable backups where you choose, or connect optional backup and Device Sync features to your own Google Drive account.

03

Your records and exports

You control your local records, backup files, and exports. Google Drive files remain in the Google Drive account you connect.

04

Limited central information

Outside account setup, authentication, support, and related service operations, Jesse K Apps does not receive or centrally store your normal accounting records.
