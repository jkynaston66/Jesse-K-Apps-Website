About Jesse K Mileage

Jesse K Mileage is provided and operated by Jesse K Apps, LLC (“Jesse K Apps,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It is an iPhone application for recording and reviewing mileage and includes optional profile, export, Jesse K Books synchronization, mortgage-calculator, resource, and contact features. The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use, Jesse K Mileage may handle precise location and route coordinates; trip dates, times, duration, distance, start and end points and addresses, detected stops, notes, business purposes, business or personal classifications, mileage rates and estimated deductions; saved places; vehicle descriptions, license-plate and odometer information; app settings and preferences; mortgage-calculator inputs and results, including ZIP code, property, loan, income, debt, tax, insurance, mortgage-insurance, and payment information; and files you generate or import through supported workflows.

The app also creates local operational information such as a random installation identifier, pending synchronization state, notification state, and a limited rolling automatic-tracking diagnostic log. The diagnostic log records state-transition names, timestamps, and limited technical details; it is designed not to include sampled route coordinates and is not exposed through the production user interface.

Optional profile registration

Creating a Jesse K Mileage profile is optional. When you save a profile or communication preference, the app sends your first and last name, email address, mobile phone number, profession, optional company or brokerage, city, state, and referral source, together with a random installation identifier, app and device information, registration and update times, and applicable consent choices and timestamps, to a Jesse K Apps registration service implemented with Google Apps Script and a private Google Sheet. This request does not include mileage records, routes, precise location, trip addresses, deductions, notes, vehicles, odometer records, calculator scenarios, or exports.

Contact by NEXA Mortgage, LLC about mortgage information, resources, and occasional marketing by call, text, or email requires a separate affirmative choice in the app. It is off by default and is not required to create a profile or use mileage features. When you save that choice, the app sends its value, date, consent version, and app source with the profile request described above. Message and data rates may apply. You can change the choice in Communication Preferences.

Historical marketing consent recorded before the NEXA-specific choice remains a separate historical record exactly as recorded. It is not automatically treated as consent to contact you on behalf of NEXA Mortgage, LLC. NEXA outreach requires the new affirmative NEXA-specific choice.

Location, motion, and automatic tracking

Manual tracking uses Core Location to obtain precise location and route samples while a trip you start is active. With the required permission, background location allows that trip to continue while the app is not in the foreground or the screen is locked. Location updates stop when tracking ends, subject to normal operating-system timing and recovery behavior.

If you enable Automatic Tracking, the app uses Core Motion motion-activity information and low-power Core Location monitoring to identify possible automotive travel. When a drive is detected, the app may begin precise background location updates, build a route, estimate distance, detect stops, end the trip under its detection rules, and ask you to classify the resulting trip. Automatic mode may operate when the app is not open and requires the permissions and background-execution capability allowed by iOS. Motion observations are used for trip-detection logic and are not sent to Jesse K Apps.

Location, motion, and automatic-detection results can be incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate. You control location and motion permissions in iOS Settings and may use manual tracking or manual trip entry instead. Disabling permissions can prevent or limit tracking.

Addresses, saved places, and maps

The app may send address-search text or coordinates to Apple system services, including MapKit, Apple Maps, and geocoding services, to provide autocomplete suggestions, readable addresses, maps, and current-location features. Apple may process those requests under its own terms and privacy practices. Jesse K Apps does not receive those Apple service requests. References to Apple services describe compatibility and do not imply Apple endorses Jesse K Mileage.

Saved-place names, addresses, and coordinates are stored with the app’s local settings. They may be used locally to label nearby trip locations and to prioritize address suggestions.

Local storage, retention, and deletion

Trip records and active-trip recovery data are stored in the app’s private on-device container using SwiftData. Route coordinates, detected stops, addresses, classifications, purposes, notes, vehicle and tax-year odometer information are stored with those records. Saved places, profile drafts, preferences, calculator sessions, installation identifiers, notification state, and synchronization queues are stored locally using app-private storage such as UserDefaults.

Business and unclassified trips remain until you delete them or remove the app, subject to device and operating-system behavior. Personal trips retained for recovery are automatically deleted approximately 30 days after classification. Deleting a trip removes it from the local app database but may not remove copies already exported, placed in Google Drive, included in device backups, or held by another recipient. Uninstalling the app ordinarily removes its local container, but Apple-managed backups or restored devices may preserve or restore app data according to your settings.

Mortgage-calculator scenarios and ZIP-based property-tax and homeowners-insurance estimates are calculated from user inputs and data bundled with the app. The current calculator stores its session locally. PDFs are generated locally in a temporary directory. Calculator inputs and PDFs are not sent to Jesse K Apps unless you choose an external contact, sharing, application, or support action.

Exports and user-directed sharing

PDF, CSV, JSON, and Jesse K Books transfer files are generated locally in temporary storage. Information leaves the app when you choose a destination through the iOS share sheet or another external action. Exported files can contain trip, route-related, address, business-purpose, note, vehicle, odometer, rate, and estimated-deduction information. You are responsible for reviewing the destination and protecting each exported copy.

Optional Jesse K Books synchronization

If you connect a Google account for Jesse K Books synchronization, the app uses Google Sign-In and requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. Eligible, completed business-trip records may then synchronize while the app is active to files in the “Jesse K Books Sync” folders created for this workflow in your Google Drive. Synchronized records may include trip and installation identifiers, dates and times, business miles, mileage rate, business purpose, notes, start and end addresses, duration, original tracked miles, manual-adjustment status, vehicle identifier, revision information, and synchronization timestamps. The app may also read acknowledgement files written by Jesse K Books.

Google controls its sign-in and Drive services, credentials, availability, retention, and security under your Google account and Google’s terms. Disconnecting Google Sign-In stops new synchronization but does not automatically delete files already stored in Google Drive. You must delete those files from Google Drive if desired. Jesse K Apps does not receive the contents of your Google Drive synchronization files through this feature.

When you connect Google Drive synchronization, Google Sign-In and related Google services may process account or device identifiers, approximate location information, and service-use information to provide sign-in, operate the connected service, and perform analytics consistent with Google’s privacy practices. Jesse K Apps does not receive or store your Google Drive synchronization files through this feature and does not use this information for cross-app tracking or advertising.

Notifications and badges

If you grant notification permission, the app schedules local alerts and may update the app badge for matters such as trip review, stationary or long-running trips, automatic-trip classification, and odometer reminders. Notification content and timing are managed through Apple’s notification system. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Other external services and actions

The app includes the Google Sign-In SDK and uses Google APIs for the optional Drive synchronization described above. It also uses Apple frameworks and services for location, motion activity, maps, address search, geocoding, notifications, PDF creation, sharing, phone, SMS, email, and opening external links. Mortgage-resource links, QR codes, contact buttons, and application links open destinations outside the app, where the destination’s terms and privacy policy apply.

The audited code does not include an advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Jesse K Apps does not use the app’s data for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not sell personal information. Apple, Google, a selected sharing destination, a linked website, or your device or network provider may receive information as necessary to perform the action you request.

Support communications

If you contact support, call, text, email, open an application destination, or follow an external link, the receiving service obtains the information you choose to provide and may receive ordinary connection information. Do not send sensitive trip, financial, authentication, or identifying information unless necessary and appropriate for the request.

Security limitations

Jesse K Mileage relies on operating-system protections and uses encrypted HTTPS connections for profile registration and Google services, but no device, storage method, transmission, or third-party service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your iPhone, device passcode, Apple and Google accounts, exported files, backups, and information shared with others.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may decline optional profile registration, NEXA Mortgage, LLC contact consent, Google Drive synchronization, notifications, motion access, or location access, although disabling a permission can prevent the related feature from operating. You can review, edit, classify, or delete local trips and saved places in the app, disconnect Google Sign-In, and delete exported or synchronized files from destinations you control.

“Delete Registration” clears the profile from the device and sends or queues a request to clear contact and profile fields in the registration sheet. The service marks the record Deleted, records the deletion time, and retains limited operational identifiers and timestamps. Historical consent cells remain exactly as recorded for consent-history purposes where retention is permitted or required; they are not active authorization for NEXA Mortgage, LLC or for outreach after deletion. The NEXA communication preference is set to No. Deleting a profile does not delete local mileage records, exported files, or files in a Google Drive account you connected. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of centrally held information by contacting support@jessekapps.com.

United States use and children

The app and its services are operated for users in the United States. Jesse K Mileage is not directed to children under 13, and Jesse K Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the app. If you believe a child supplied information, contact us.
