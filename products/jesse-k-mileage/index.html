Mileage tracking that stays out of your way

Automatic mileage tracking, without the busywork.

Jesse K Mileage is a free mileage tracker app for iPhone that helps you capture drives, separate business from personal and volunteer mileage with a swipe, and keep a clear trip history when it is time to report.

Android · Coming Soon

The iPhone app is available now. Android remains in development and has no public download.

Built for the full trip workflow

From motion to mileage records.

01

Automatic mileage tracking

When you explicitly enable automatic tracking and the required permissions, the app can detect qualifying drives for later review.

02

Swipe to classify

Move through unclassified trips with a simple swipe and mark each drive as Business, Personal, or Volunteer / Charity.

03

Trip history

Review saved drives, dates, distance, route details, notes, vehicle information, and mileage adjustments in one place.

04

Mileage reporting

Create tax-year summaries and user-controlled PDF, CSV, and Jesse K Books JSON exports after reviewing your records.

05

Manual control when needed

Start and end a trip yourself, add a missed drive, or correct mileage so your records reflect what actually happened.

06

Simple everyday setup

Choose how you want to track, grant only the permissions needed for that workflow, then review trips on your schedule.
