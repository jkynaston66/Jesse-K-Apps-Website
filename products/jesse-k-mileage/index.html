FAQ

Jesse K Mileage questions.

Is Jesse K Mileage free?

Yes. Jesse K Mileage is 100% free, with no subscription required.

Does it automatically track drives?

It can detect eligible drives when automatic tracking and the required permissions are enabled, but detection is not guaranteed.

Where are my trips stored?

Trip records are stored locally on your device. Review the Privacy Policy for details about optional services and other information.

Can I manually add a trip?

Yes. You can add trips manually and edit records when needed.

Can I export my mileage?

Yes. You can create exports and control where those files are stored or sent.

Does the app guarantee IRS-compliant records?

No. You are responsible for reviewing records and determining whether they satisfy applicable tax requirements.

Does it need location access?

Automatic tracking requires location access. You control permissions through your device settings.

Does it work in the background?

Background location capabilities can support automatic tracking when enabled, subject to device settings, operating-system behavior, and the limitations described in the User Agreement.
