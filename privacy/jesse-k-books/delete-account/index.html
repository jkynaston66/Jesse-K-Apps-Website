Before you request deletion

Deleting your Jesse K Books sign-in ends access to that account. If other Books editions use the same account, sign-in may also stop there. Back up or export what you need from every business you manage first. Keep the password for an encrypted backup separately. Removing the account cannot recover a missing backup or a forgotten backup password.

Send your request

Email support@jessekapps.com with the subject “Delete my Jesse K Books account.” Send from your sign-in address if possible. If you have lost access to it, say so and ask for another way to verify your identity. Do not include a password, receipt image, backup, tax number or financial record. There is no fee to request deletion.

What we remove

After verification, we remove the Firebase sign-in record and delete associated registration and other account data we control, except information we may lawfully retain for limited security, compliance, disputes or proof of the request. We will explain applicable exceptions. Google’s backup and security systems may retain limited records under its retention practices. We will complete a verified request within 30 calendar days, or sooner if required by law. If a lawful extension is necessary, we will explain why and when we expect completion, when permitted.

Remove local files and copies separately

To remove the private data on an Android device, use Android Settings, open Apps, select Jesse K Books, then select Storage and clear the app’s storage. You may also uninstall the app. Menu names can vary by device. Either action can erase all books, customers and receipts stored by that installation. Save needed records first. Delete exported files in each destination you chose, including your own Google Drive, and manage copies already shared with other people. Uninstalling or disconnecting a storage service does not itself send us an account-deletion request.
