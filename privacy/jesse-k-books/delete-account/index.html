Jesse K Books for Android

Delete an account

Request deletion of the Firebase Authentication account used to sign in to Jesse K Books for Android.

On this page Before requesting Submit a request What is deleted Local data Timing

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Before requesting deletion

Deleting the authentication account stops future sign-in. Before submitting a request, export an encrypted backup of any records you need to keep. Jesse K Apps cannot recover the account, device-local records, or a forgotten backup password after deletion.

Submit a deletion request

  1. Email support@jessekapps.com with the subject “Jesse K Books Android account deletion request.”
  2. Send the request from the email address used to sign in to Jesse K Books. If that mailbox is unavailable, explain that in the request so Jesse K Apps can provide another reasonable verification method.
  3. Do not send a password, receipt, backup, accounting record, taxpayer identifier, or other unnecessary financial information.

What Jesse K Apps deletes

After verification, Jesse K Apps deletes the Firebase Authentication user record associated with the requesting email address, including the Firebase user identifier and Email/Password sign-in access. The current Android application does not store accounting records or receipt contents in a Jesse K Apps cloud database.

Remove information stored on the device

Jesse K Apps cannot remotely access or erase the app-private accounting database, customer records, receipt images, or receipt drafts stored on an Android device. To erase them, use Android Settings to clear Jesse K Books storage or uninstall the app. Delete exported backup files directly from every destination where they were saved.

Timing and limited retention

Jesse K Apps will complete a verified deletion request within 30 calendar days. If applicable law permits or requires additional time or retention, Jesse K Apps will notify the requester when permitted and explain the reason.

A limited record of the request and completion may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Google may retain limited service records under its applicable Firebase terms and retention practices.
