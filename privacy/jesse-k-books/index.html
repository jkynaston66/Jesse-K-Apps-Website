Jesse K Books for Android

Jesse K Books Android Privacy Supplement

How the Android application handles authentication, local accounting records, receipts, on-device text recognition, and encrypted backups.

Revision dated November 18, 2026

Scope

This supplement covers Jesse K Books for Android (com.jessekapps.books). Read it with the Books Privacy Policy. This supplement controls where Android handles information differently. Jesse K Apps, LLC is responsible for the app. It is intended for adults in the United States.

Your local records

When you enter a transaction, add a customer or capture a receipt, the working records and attachments are kept in app-private device storage. Your books are separated using an identifier derived from the signed-in account. This edition has production synchronization disabled. It does not send your ledger or receipt images to a cloud accounting database. Turning off synchronization does not prevent the sign-in and diagnostic traffic described below.

Signing in

This Android edition uses an existing Firebase email and password account. It has no in-app account creation or Google or Microsoft federated login. Google handles credentials and tokens and processes the email, account ID, IP address, app ID, platform and SDK information needed for authentication, security and abuse prevention. Your plaintext password is not kept in the ledger. Google may process account and service data where it operates.

Camera and receipt recognition

The device’s camera app supplies the image to Books through a temporary file grant. Books uses the bundled Google ML Kit reader on your device. It extracts fields such as merchant, date, tax and total from the image. Books keeps the receipt and selected fields locally and does not retain the full recognized text after extraction. Check those fields before posting.

Google’s SDK can send information about the app, device, installation, feature configuration, input and output sizes, errors, events and performance. These service diagnostics are separate from the receipt image and text used for recognition. Jesse K Apps does not use this information for cross-site advertising or sell it. This edition does not use an advertising SDK, behavioral analytics, a bank connection or a payment processor.

Backups and restore

Android’s manual backup encrypts the accounting database and attachments with a password you choose. The backup format uses PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 for key derivation and AES-256-GCM for encryption and integrity. You pick where to save the encrypted file. The destination may be local or a storage service you choose. Jesse K Apps does not retain the backup password or a recovery copy. Keep the password separately and test a restore. The working database, a separately generated report and the encrypted backup are different files. Do not assume that every file you export has backup protection. Automatic Android backup and device-to-device transfer of private app data are disabled in this edition.

Retention and access

An accounting reversal preserves the original posting history. Local files remain until removed using the app or Android storage controls. Deleting sign-in stops future authentication and can make existing books inaccessible. It does not remotely erase files on the device. Export needed records before deleting your account. Clearing app storage and uninstalling can remove all local business records on that installation. Separately remove saved backups and shared copies where you control them. The app rejects cleartext network traffic. Device protections and encrypted backups reduce risks but cannot guarantee security.

Deleting an account

Use the Android account-deletion instructions at https://jessekapps.com/privacy/jesse-k-books/delete-account/ or email support@jessekapps.com. We verify identity, delete the account and associated personal data under our control, and explain any legally permitted retention. We will complete verified deletion requests within 30 calendar days, or sooner if required. If a lawful extension or retention applies, we will tell you the reason and timing when permitted. Google may retain limited service records for security, backup or legal purposes.

Other requests and changes

The Books Privacy Policy explains support, access, correction, lawful disclosures, security incidents and changes. We will notify you of material changes and obtain consent where required.
