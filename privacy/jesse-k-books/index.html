Jesse K Books for Android

Privacy Policy

How the Android application handles authentication, local accounting records, receipts, on-device text recognition, and encrypted backups.

On this page Scope Local data Authentication Receipt OCR Backups Retention Account deletion Contact

Effective date: October 23, 2026

1. Scope and developer

This policy applies to the Jesse K Books Android application, package com.jessekapps.books. Jesse K Books is provided by Jesse K Apps, LLC. This Android policy supplements the broader Jesse K Books product privacy policy. This Android policy controls where it describes Android-specific behavior differently.

Jesse K Books is business recordkeeping software for adults. It is not directed to children.

2. Accounting and receipt information stored locally

Accounts, balanced journal transactions, customer names and contact details, receipt images, receipt drafts, extracted receipt fields, reports, and related bookkeeping records are stored in the application's private storage on the Android device. Extracted receipt fields may include merchant, date, total, and tax. The app does not retain the complete raw text recognized from a receipt after extracting those fields.

Production synchronization is disabled. The current Android release does not upload accounting records, customer records, receipt contents, or receipt images to a Jesse K Apps server, Firebase database, Google Drive synchronization service, or other cloud accounting database.

Receipt capture opens the Android system camera application and receives the resulting image through a temporary secure file grant. Jesse K Books does not request direct Android camera permission.

3. Firebase Authentication

The release build requires an existing Email/Password account. The Android app does not currently provide self-service account creation. Google and Microsoft federated sign-in are not enabled.

Google Firebase Authentication processes the sign-in email address, a Firebase user identifier, Internet Protocol address, Firebase Android App ID, device platform, and Authentication SDK user-agent information for sign-in, account administration, security, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention. Firebase manages authentication credentials and tokens. Jesse K Books does not store the account's plaintext password in its local accounting database.

Local books are separated by an identifier derived from the authenticated Firebase user identifier. Firebase authentication information may be processed where Google and its service providers operate, subject to Google's applicable Firebase terms and privacy documentation.

4. On-device receipt text recognition

Jesse K Books uses the bundled Google ML Kit text-recognition SDK. Receipt images and recognized receipt text are processed on the device and are not sent to Google as text-recognition feature input.

Google states that ML Kit may transmit limited device and application information, a per-installation identifier, API configuration, feature versions, input and output sizes, event types, error codes, and performance metrics for service operation, diagnostics, analytics, maintenance, security, and abuse prevention. Google states that this diagnostic information is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties.

5. Encrypted backups and user-directed transfers

You may export an encrypted backup through Android's system document picker to a location you select. A backup can include the local accounting database and attached receipt files. Jesse K Books encrypts the backup using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password derivation and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.

The backup password is held temporarily while you perform the export or restore. It is not persistently stored by Jesse K Books or sent to Jesse K Apps. Jesse K Apps cannot recover the password or decrypt the backup. The storage provider and destination you select may process the encrypted file under their own terms. You control exported files and any copies you share.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for the application's private data.

6. Sharing, advertising, and sales

Jesse K Apps does not sell personal or business information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The Android app contains no advertising SDK, bank connection, payment processing, general behavioral analytics SDK, Firebase accounting database, or production synchronization service.

Firebase Authentication and ML Kit process the limited information described above as service providers used to provide authentication, diagnostics, maintenance, and security. A user-directed encrypted backup transfer is made to the destination the user selects.

7. Retention and security

Local information remains on the Android device until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Reversing a posted accounting transaction preserves the accounting audit history rather than deleting the original entry. Jesse K Apps cannot remotely access or erase the device-local database. Exported backups remain in the selected destination until the user deletes them.

Firebase Authentication account information remains in the Firebase project until the account is deleted, subject to limited provider retention for security, fraud prevention, backups, or legal compliance.

The application uses app-private Android storage, disables automatic Android backup and device transfer, rejects cleartext network traffic, separates local books by authenticated user, and uses authenticated encryption for manual backups. Network service providers use encrypted transport. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Account deletion

To request deletion of the Firebase Authentication account used by Jesse K Books, follow the steps on the Jesse K Books account-deletion page.

Jesse K Apps will complete a verified deletion request within 30 calendar days. If applicable law permits or requires additional time or retention, Jesse K Apps will notify the requester when permitted and explain the reason. Deletion stops future sign-in with that account.

Account deletion cannot remotely erase information stored only on the user's device or in a user-selected backup destination. Before requesting deletion, export any backup the user needs. To erase local information, clear Jesse K Books storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Delete exported backup files directly from their storage destination.

Jesse K Apps may retain a limited record of the deletion request and completion when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the Android application, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised. Material changes will receive notice or consent when required.
