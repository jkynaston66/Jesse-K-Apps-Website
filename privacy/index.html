Website Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current information practices of jessekapps.com.

On this page Scope Current practices Third parties Product policies Contact

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

This Website Privacy Policy applies to the Jesse K Apps, LLC website at https://jessekapps.com (the “Website”). Jesse K Apps, LLC is referred to as “Jesse K Apps,” “we,” “us,” or “our.”

1. Scope

This policy applies only to the Website. Individual Jesse K Apps software products may have separate privacy policies describing product-specific accounts, local storage, backups, connected services, and other data practices. A product-specific policy controls for that product where it differs from this Website policy.

2. Current Website practices

The current Website is a static informational website. It does not provide user accounts, ecommerce, payment processing, forms, application backends, comments, or direct file uploads. Jesse K Apps does not use the Website to request or centrally store accounting records, financial records, payroll data, customer information, receipts, documents, mileage records, mortgage scenarios, or other product data.

3. Information you choose to provide

If you contact Jesse K Apps through an email application or another destination reached from a Website link, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, contact details, message, screenshots, diagnostics, or attachments. Do not send passwords, full Social Security numbers, full taxpayer identification numbers, government-site credentials, or unnecessary financial records. We may retain communications as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain security, document a request, and meet legal obligations.

4. Hosting and technical information

The Website may be hosted or delivered by a third-party infrastructure provider. Like most web infrastructure, that provider may automatically process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the Website, such as an Internet Protocol address, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security or diagnostic logs. Such processing is subject to the provider’s own terms and privacy practices. Jesse K Apps does not currently operate an application backend for this Website.

5. Cookies, analytics, advertising, and artificial intelligence

The current Website does not intentionally set advertising cookies, conduct cross-context behavioral advertising, or include an account-based analytics system. Jesse K Apps does not use information submitted through this Website to train artificial-intelligence models. If Website practices materially change, this policy will be updated and notice or consent will be provided where appropriate or legally required.

6. External links and third-party services

The Website may link to app stores, support destinations, email applications, software downloads, or other third-party websites and services. Third parties control their own terms, privacy practices, security, accuracy, availability, and data handling. Following an external link may allow that third party to receive information under its own policies. Jesse K Apps does not control those practices.

7. Disclosures for law, security, and business transfers

Jesse K Apps may disclose limited information it holds to service providers that help operate hosting, support, or security; when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, a valid subpoena, court order, or other enforceable legal process; to investigate fraud, abuse, unlawful activity, or security threats; to protect users, the public, Jesse K Apps, or others; or in connection with a legitimate merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale or transfer of Jesse K Apps. Advance notice may not be provided when prohibited by law. A business transfer is not the sale of personal information as a standalone data product, and transferred information remains subject to this policy and applicable law.

8. Security and retention

Jesse K Apps uses reasonable measures appropriate to the information and Website functions it operates, but no website, provider, device, storage system, or transmission can guarantee absolute security. Information voluntarily sent to us may be retained as reasonably necessary for the purpose provided, security, dispute resolution, compliance, and legal obligations.

9. Children

The Website is intended for a general business audience and is not directed to children under 13. Jesse K Apps does not knowingly use the Website to collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Product-specific privacy policies

Product features and data practices are not governed solely by this Website policy. Review the applicable product policy before using a Jesse K Apps application.

11. Your requests

You may ask about, request access to, request correction of, or request deletion of personal information centrally held by Jesse K Apps by contacting support@jessekapps.com. We may take reasonable steps to verify identity and authority before acting and may retain information where reasonably necessary or permitted by law.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Website, law, services, and information practices change. The current revision date appears above. Material changes affecting how personal information is collected, used, disclosed, or shared will receive clear notice and consent where appropriate or legally required.

13. Contact
