Jesse K Apps Website Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current information practices of jessekapps.com.

On this page Scope Browsing and technical data Disclosure and retention Product notices Changes and contact

Revision dated November 18, 2026

Scope. Jesse K Apps, LLC is responsible for the public company and product-information pages at jessekapps.com. Product sign-in, accounting, storage, app permissions and communications inside an application are covered by its product notice. External sites such as a mortgage application destination have their own notices.

Browsing and technical data. When you request a page or download, the site’s hosting and delivery providers may process your IP address, device or browser information, requested path, date and time, and connection, diagnostic or security information. We use these services to deliver pages, troubleshoot errors and protect the site. Browsing the informational pages does not submit your bookkeeping files, payroll or receipts to us.

When you contact us. A support or email link may open another application. If you send a message, we and our email service receive your address, message and any attachments. We use them to respond and document the request. Please omit passwords, tax identifiers, unredacted payroll and any financial record that we do not need. A link to another independent site may disclose normal connection information to that destination.

Tracking and advertising. The informational pages do not intentionally set advertising cookies. We do not use information from these informational pages to sell personal data or serve ads based on your activity across unrelated websites. We do not authorize advertising partners to collect visitors’ browsing histories here for that purpose. Our treatment of cross-site ad tracking is the same when a browser sends a Do Not Track signal. Hosting and security services may still process the technical information needed for their work. We do not use support information to train AI models.

Disclosure and retention. We may provide information to services that supply hosting, security or support, when required by lawful process, to address abuse or protect legal rights, or as part of a legitimate business transfer subject to applicable law. Technical information is used to operate and secure the website. Support records are kept as needed for support, security, compliance and dispute resolution. Provider retention and backup cycles may differ from the retention of our active records.

Requests and children. Email support@jessekapps.com to ask for access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold or to raise a concern about a response. We may verify identity and authority. Additional rights may apply where you live. The website is intended for a general business audience. It is not directed to children under 13. If a child supplied personal information, contact us.

Product notices. Read the Books Privacy Policy, Android Privacy Supplement or Android Account Deletion instructions. Other product notices are available through the product directory.

Changes and contact. We will identify the date of revisions and provide notice of material changes and consent where required. Contact support@jessekapps.com or Jesse K Apps, LLC, 7090 N Oracle Rd, Ste 178 PMB 1265, Tucson, AZ 85704.
